# myICOR

**Warm tradition meets tech precision.** myICOR is an AI-powered Personal Knowledge Assistance system built on the ICOR methodology. It runs in any LLM you already use, keeps your knowledge in plain markdown, and stays on your machine. Yours forever.

## What lives here

This organization hosts the public scaffold, the optional expansion packs, and the small ecosystem of tools and skills that ship around myICOR. The product itself lives at [myicor.com](https://myicor.com).

## Start with myPKA

[**myPKA**](https://github.com/myICOR/myPKA) is the open scaffold. A working AI team in a folder, structured around ICOR. No database, no SaaS, no vendor. Drop the folder, point your LLM at it, and a six-person team is online.

- Works with Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, Cursor, Obsidian, ChatGPT, and any LLM that reads `AGENTS.md`.
- Plain markdown end to end. Greppable, portable, version-controllable.
- Upgrades to SQLite when the folder gets large.
- Imports from Notion, Obsidian, Heptabase, Roam, Logseq, Tana, Apple Notes, Evernote.

[Read the scaffold](https://github.com/myICOR/myPKA) · [Watch the walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4C2w8eIG48A) · [Sign up at myicor.com](https://myicor.com)

## About the methodology

ICOR is the framework Paperless Movement and thousands of professionals run their lives and businesses on. Four workstreams, four contexts, one operating model: Personal Knowledge Management, Personal Project Management, Business Knowledge Management, Business Project Management. Every folder in myPKA, every routing decision, every specialist contract maps to a piece of that framework. The structure is not arbitrary. The reasoning is teachable.
